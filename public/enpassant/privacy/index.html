En Passant privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026.

En Passant keeps your chess, your settings and your choices on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. The only information that leaves your phone is what's needed to sell and restore Pro.

What stays on your phone

Everything below is stored only on your device. We (the developer) never receive it.

  • Your chess: your level, ratings, puzzles seen, games, opening progress, the day's game clock, and your progress and weekly totals.
  • The apps you block: which apps you chose.
  • Your profile: the name and photo you add, if you add them. The photo is a small copy of the one you pick in your phone's photo picker; En Passant can't see the rest of your photos.
  • Your settings: board style, clock settings, and whether the weekly recap is on.

Your phone's own backup (Google's Android backup, or iCloud) may include this data, under your account with Google or Apple. You can turn that off in your phone's settings.

Permissions, and what they're used for

  • Accessibility Service (Android). En Passant uses it for one thing: to notice which app has just opened, so it can put a chess challenge in front of the apps you've chosen to block. It sees only the name of the app that's opening. It doesn't read what's on your screen, what you type, or anything inside other apps. Nothing it sees is stored, sent anywhere, or shared. You're asked before it's turned on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  • Screen Time (iPhone). En Passant uses Apple's Screen Time framework to block the apps you pick. Apple keeps your choices private: En Passant never learns which apps they are or how you use them.
  • Usage access (Android, optional). If you allow it, En Passant reads how long you've used each app over the last 7 days, to sort the list of apps and suggest which to block. It's read on your phone, only while you're choosing apps, and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications (optional). For the weekly recap and the reminder before your free week of Pro ends. They're made on your phone; nothing is sent to a server to create them.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Google Play or the App Store, which handle payment. We never see your card or payment details.

To check whether you have Pro on any of your devices, En Passant uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which plan you bought, when, and its receipt) together with a random identifier made for En Passant. That identifier isn't linked to your name, email address or any advertising identifier. RevenueCat processes this on our behalf, to validate purchases and show us totals such as how many people subscribe. It's sent encrypted. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of your information.
  • No accounts.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling En Passant deletes everything it stored on your phone. To have your purchase history deleted from RevenueCat, contact us at matty@mattysun.com and we'll delete it. (Your purchase stays on record with Google or Apple, under their own policies.)

Children

En Passant isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date, and the app will point to it.
